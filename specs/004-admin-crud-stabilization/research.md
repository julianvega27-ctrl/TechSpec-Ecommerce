# Research Findings

- **Dashboards UI**: We will build simple card-based layouts in React, reusing our `[var(--color-surface)]` and `[var(--color-primary)]` tokens to maintain aesthetic consistency. We do not need heavy chart libraries (like Recharts) right now; plain metric cards and simple tables for recent orders will satisfy the "basic dashboards" requirement.
- **Dynamic Home Settings**: We will load settings dynamically on the client side in `Hero.tsx`.

No further unknowns remain.
